How I Work

My Design Process

Every project follows a rigorous cycle — grounded in real user research, shaped by business constraints, and always validated before shipping.

01
🔍
Discover

User interviews, remote usability tests, surveys, and stakeholder alignment to uncover the real pain points.

02
🎯
Define

Personas, journey maps, IA restructuring, and problem framing — turning raw insights into a clear direction.

03
✏️
Prototype

Wireframes through hi-fidelity, component libraries, and interactive prototypes built for testing.

04
🔄
Validate

Usability testing with real users, SUS scoring, data-informed iterations, and stakeholder trade-off decisions.

About Me

Design is how I think,
not just what I do

I'm a Lead UX Designer at Vision Group Inc, designing AI/ML and IoT-powered retail execution products used by enterprise clients across Europe. My work sits at the intersection of user research, product strategy, and craft — I care deeply about the gap between how people are expected to use a product and how they actually do.

I work across the full design cycle — from unmoderated user testing and card sorting to hi-fidelity prototypes and design system components. When I'm not designing, I'm with my family, who remind me every day why human-centred thinking matters.
