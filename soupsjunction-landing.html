🇬🇭 From Ghana

As a teenager, Stella Amoako would wake up early and walk the markets of Ghana with her mother — picking out the freshest tomatoes, groundnuts, and spices for the family's daily meals. Those morning rituals shaped everything she knows about food.

Years later, she founded Soups Junction to bring that same energy into kitchens everywhere. Her ebooks translate traditional Ghanaian recipes into clear, approachable guides — so you can cook the real thing, wherever you are in the world.
