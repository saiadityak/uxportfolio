Who We Are

COMPOSITE
EXCELLENCE
Built For The Gulf

VMS Composites is a UAE-based manufacturer specializing in Glass Reinforced Polyester (GRP/FRP) products and portable modular solutions. From ruggedized electrical enclosures to luxury portable sanitation units, every product we make is engineered to perform in the harshest Gulf conditions — salt air, desert heat, and demanding project timelines.

We serve major contractors, government bodies, oil & gas operators, event organizers, and developers across the GCC, delivering custom-built solutions that combine international-grade quality with fast regional turnaround.
